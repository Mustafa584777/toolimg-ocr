ToolIMG Billing & Guarantees Last Updated: August 6, 2026

This Refund Policy 2 document serves as the supplementary terms governing subscription auto-renewals, credit metering calculations, service SLA guarantees, and chargeback dispute resolutions for ToolIMG (https://toolimg.online).

1. Purpose & Scope of Policy 2

While our primary Refund Policy provides the core 14-day money-back guarantee, this extended Policy 2 governs complex billing situations including enterprise API tiers, automated recurring charges, partial credit utilization, and payment gateway chargeback scenarios.

2. Subscription Auto-Renewal Refunds

ToolIMG subscription plans automatically renew at the beginning of each billing cycle unless cancelled prior to the renewal date. If your account is auto-renewed and you did not intend to continue:

  • You must notify support@toolimg.online within 48 hours of the renewal charge.
  • No credits allocated in the new billing period must have been consumed for OCR scanning, prompt generation, or code conversion tools.

3. Credit Metering & Partial Refunds

ToolIMG tracks credit consumption accurately in real-time. Unused portions of credit packages may be eligible for partial refunds under special circumstances evaluated by our support team on a case-by-case basis.

4. Chargeback & Payment Dispute Prevention

We strongly encourage users to contact our support team before initiating a chargeback or payment dispute with their bank or credit card provider. Most billing concerns can be resolved within 24 hours without administrative payment holds.

5. SLA Uptime & Service Failure Compensation

If ToolIMG core processing engines suffer extended unplanned downtime exceeding 99.0% availability in a billing month, impacted paid subscribers are eligible for pro-rated account credits or refund adjustments.
