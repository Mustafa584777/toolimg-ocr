Editorial Policy

ToolIMG Guidelines Last Updated: August 5, 2026

At ToolIMG, accessible at https://toolimg.online, we are dedicated to providing developers, designers, and writers with precise, ethical, and high-performance image-to-text, OCR, and code conversion tools. This Editorial Policy defines our commitment to technological integrity, transparency, and quality management.

1. Mission & Quality Standards

ToolIMG strives to bridge the gap between static design layouts, handwritten archives, and dynamic digital assets. We follow a rigorous approach to software quality to ensure that the content, code, and text extracted through our web tools represent top-tier technical precision.

No Hidden Proxies: We always perform data translation and transcription transparently without altering core text meanings.
Semantic Preservation: Retaining original layouts, spacing, indentation, and typographic markers in converted HTML/CSS codes.

2. Accuracy & Objective Evaluation

Because automated vision models operate on probabilities, extracted texts can sometimes diverge from the original handwritten scripts. To mitigate errors:

  • We benchmark our English and Hindi OCR pipelines weekly using diverse test datasets to optimize transcription confidence levels.
  • We encourage users to inspect high-contrast or poorly lit documents manually and review extracted drafts before publishing or committing them to codebases.

3. Responsible Use of Generative AI

ToolIMG leverages state-of-the-art Large Language Models (LLMs) and Multimodal Vision APIs (such as Google Gemini) to reverse-engineer layout grids, formulate prompts from references, and convert mockups to responsive scripts.

We are dedicated to preventing the dissemination of biased, misleading, or synthetic content. Our prompts are strictly designed to serve functional utility tasks, producing pure structural code blocks and authentic literal transcribing outputs.

4. Cultural Inclusivity & Script Integrity

We believe technology should represent global scripts and languages with equal performance. Recognizing the visual complexity of Devnagari (Hindi) handwritten characters, we have dedicated specialized engineering assets to ensure that our Hindi Handwriting OCR supports cursive ligatures, matras, and hybrid script annotations seamlessly.

5. Data Privacy & Transparency

As part of our commitment to safety, our image-to-text algorithms follow strict ephemeral data regulations:

Privacy Safeguard Commitment

HOW WE MANAGE UPLOADED INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY:

Client-side conversions run entirely sandbox-secure in your browser engine without sending bytes to third-party endpoints.
Cloud API-driven transcriptions enforce short retention bounds, automatically clearing uploaded design templates and handwritten notes shortly after transaction finality.

6. Feedback & Technical Updates

Technology is a living ecosystem. When transcription failures or code-compilation bugs are discovered, our engineering desk acts with urgency to repair underlying nodes. If you identify a systematic translation bias, layout breakdown, or OCR glitch, please reach out with details to: support@toolimg.online
