Last Updated: June 23, 2026

At ToolImg (“we”, “us”, or “our”), your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Notice explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our website at https://toolimg.online.

We collect minimal information to provide and improve our services:

  • Device Information: We may collect information about your browser type, operating system, and device type for analytics purposes.
  • Cookies: We use cookies and similar tracking technologies. See Section 3 for more details.
Important Client-Side Notice: Most of our image processing tools run entirely in your browser (client-side). Your images and files are never uploaded to our servers. They remain on your device at all times.
