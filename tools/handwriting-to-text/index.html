Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How accurate is the handwriting to text conversion?

ToolIMG is powered by modern multimodal AI vision models that deliver exceptional accuracy when transcribing handwritten notes, cursive scripts, letters, and documents.

How do I convert my handwritten notes to text?

Simply upload an image of your handwritten text, click the "Extract Text" button, and our system will generate clean, copyable digital text in a few seconds.

Can this AI handle cursive or messy handwriting?

Yes, our neural network models are specifically trained to recognize connected script, cursive documents, print letters, and complex handwritten alignments.

Is my uploaded image secure?

Yes, we take security very seriously. All uploaded files are transmitted securely using encryption and are automatically removed from our system after transcription.

Why should I digitize my notes using AI?

Converting physical paper records into digital format saves substantial manual typing time, eliminates typographical mistakes, and creates fully searchable document archives.
